The Best Movies According to Ankita

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Iron Man

A thrilling and smart superhero

Thor

An entertaining superhero origin story that follows an arrogant Asgardian prince as he learns humility and worthiness after being banished to Earth

The Pursuit of Happiness

A deeply moving and inspiring biographical drama about a struggling single father who overcomes homelessness and extreme hardship to build a better life for his young son
